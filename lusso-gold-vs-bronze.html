If we go gold, which range?

This is the real advantage of brushed gold — three quite different looks in the same finish. Prices below are basin tap, floorstanding bath filler and shower.

Modern · cheapest · widest choice

Bronze isn't the expensive option — it's the middle one. Baroque sits £225 above Luxe and £150 above Regal, but £300 below Urban. Regal is the fairest comparison: same traditional crosshead look, £150 less than Baroque, with the bath filler priced identically at £597 in both.

Before we decide

Order the finish samples first

LUSSO sell small brassware samples of each finish. Worth having both on the windowsill for a week before committing — bronze in particular changes a lot depending on the light.

Bronze won't match perfectly

It's hand-tumbled, and LUSSO say outright that tone and patina vary between items and batches, with no guaranteed match across taps, showers and screens. Some people love that; worth knowing it's deliberate, not a fault.

Bronze has a full accessory set

Baroque does towel rail £132, towel shelf £197, robe hook £77, soap dispenser £97, roll holder £77 and toilet brush £137 — all matching. Add roughly £700 if we want the lot.

Wall-mounted means first-fix

Wall-mounted taps cost £100–£175 more and the pipework has to be buried in the wall, so it's a decision for before the plastering, not after.

Two prices to confirm

Urban's floorstanding bath filler shows around £897 across different pages, and Urban's shower runs from £597 for the plain system to £997 for the thermostatic-with-handheld. Worth a call before totalling Urban up properly.

Delivery and holding

Small parcels £9.95, pallets £40–£96. They'll hold an order for 60 days on a 30% deposit, and there's a phone/trade discount worth asking about before ordering online.
